# PRIVACY

- **Where data lives:** only in Chrome's storage on your phone, for the JARVIS site. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no AI service.
- **What is stored:** tasks, memories you approve, contacts you add, settings, activity log, usage counters, backup history.
- **What leaves the phone:**
  - Voice commands: Chrome on Android sends speech audio to Google's speech service to produce text. Shown before you enable voice. Turn off anytime (System → Voice).
  - When you tap Open/Search/Calendar/WhatsApp, that app or site receives what you chose to send (e.g. the message text).
  - Fonts load from Google Fonts on first visit (then cached).
- **Microphone:** active only while the mic button is red and the "MIC LIVE" chip shows. Never in the background.
- **Privacy modes:** Normal (speaks, full reminders) · Private (speaks, generic notifications) · Public (speaks only "details on screen", generic notifications) · Meeting (silent, generic). Silent switch: never speaks.
- **Your controls:** inspect/edit/delete memory; disable memory; export or clear the log; backup/export everything; erase all.
